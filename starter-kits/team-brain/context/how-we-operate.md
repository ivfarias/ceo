## Inbox processing rhythm

- Raw notes go into `inbox/`
- Inbox is processed into structured docs on a fixed cadence
- Processed raw notes move to `inbox/processed/`

## Escalation triggers

- A KPI is off-track for two consecutive checkpoints
- A blocker is unresolved after the agreed SLA
- A customer-facing issue affects acquisition, activation, revenue, or trust
- A decision changes scope, priority, or spend in a material way
